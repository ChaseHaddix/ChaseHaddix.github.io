This technical memorandum describes the evaluation and downselection of miniature X-ray systems for future spaceflight applications. A comprehensive ground demonstration evaluated portable X-ray technologies across clinical, engineering, and science applications and used a multi-criteria decision-making approach to identify a system suitable for future flight demonstration and long-duration exploration missions.
