Research & Technology

NASA Glenn Tests Mini-X-Ray Technology to Advance Space Health Care

NASA | September 2025

NASA featured our work evaluating portable X-ray systems for future long-duration space missions. The article discusses potential medical and engineering applications of compact radiography and the work being conducted at NASA Glenn with clinical and technical collaborators.

Read the NASA feature

USRA Staff Helps NASA Glenn Pioneer Mini X-Ray Technology to Advance Space Health Care

Universities Space Research Association | October 2025

USRA highlighted my involvement in NASA Glenn's evaluation of handheld X-ray technology for future exploration missions. The work examines whether compact radiography systems can support both medical diagnostics and technical inspection needs during missions far from Earth.

Read the USRA feature

EMOTIV NeuroDiscovery Grant

EMOTIV | 2026

EMOTIV featured my NeuroDiscovery Grant project investigating cognitive workload and fatigue during simulated mission procedures. The study uses portable EEG to explore brain-based measures that may help characterize performance under demanding operational conditions, with potential applications both in spaceflight and on Earth.

View the EMOTIV feature
